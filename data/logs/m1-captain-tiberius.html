Deployed to the Loppian System in search of arcane technology, the Dark Prime Brother’s Raptors 4th Company made first contact with the enemy on the toxic world of Okamec. What began as a quick mission to establish a comms relay station quickly escalated into a pitched battle as the Raptors fought against the Axiom cult and High Elf warbands.

Leading a lightning strike, Captain Tiberius and his elite destroyer retinue drew first blood, instantly shredding a High Elf APC with their power fists before the passengers could even disembark. Supported by a squad of Prime Brothers, the destroyers laid waste to anyone foolish enough to come within range of their fists. Their regenerative abilities allowed them to shrug off hits and wounds that would kill any normal man. Only one brother was lost over the course of the battle. Thanks to a heroic stand by the Prime Brothers, a charge by the Axiom cultists was stopped at the last second, leaving the southern relay in the Raptor’s control.

In the center of the battlefield, Lieutenant Commodus and the brothers under his command fared far worse. They quickly established a position in the ruins containing the second relay but were surrounded by wretched xenos. Despite their best efforts, they could not hold off the assaults of the Axiom cult and Elves. The cult deployed a previously unseen aircraft and quickly established air superiority, and strafed our units while we could muster no response. Fighting until the very end, the brothers made the enemy pay for every inch of ground with their blood. Only two destroyer brothers survived the battle for the center after fighting their way through the carnage to the northern relay and punching their way through a cult APC.

Before extracting, we recovered data-fragments from the contested central relay indicating the location of a Voidcrown shard upon Ontranis.

While not the victory we were hoping for, we managed to fight the Axiom cult to a stalemate before extracting. We have found another clue to the technology we are searching for and will be ready to avenge our fallen brothers when we cross paths again.

— Captain Tiberius, Raptors 4th Company
