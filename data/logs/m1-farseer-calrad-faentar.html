From the poisoned skies of this rotting paradise, I, Farseer Calrad Faentar of the Hamm-Saan Fleet, send this account.

The miasma scattered our descent into disorder, yet even dispersed the blades of the Asuryani found their mark with a precision the lesser races could never match. Under my own hand, our jet bikers struck with cold elegance, felling an entire squad of the Dark Prime Brothers’ annihilators in a single, decisive charge. The revenants of our host swept the field clean of the Axiom cult’s gun drones — every last one annihilated. Even their lumbering hover transport was brought low before it could anchor their line.

The cultists of the Silent Axiom answered with crude desperation. Their heavy destroyers ambushed and destroyed our anti-grav APC in a single charge. Without its stable platform, our Elemental protectors were denied proper footing and their full strength was blunted. Still we fought. Still we prevailed where it mattered.

Despite the three-sided press of inferiors and the scattered nature of our landing, we claimed the northern comms spire. Though it lay dormant, our farseers tracked the movements of the lesser races’ vessels in low orbit. From this we deduced they are racing toward Ontranis. While the cultists and destroyers scramble through the miasma, the Hamm-Saan shall take the swift path of the Webway and arrive first.

We fought with the grace of a dying empire. They clawed and brawled in the rot like beasts. The Voidsun grows heavier with every drop of blood, but it is we who see furthest.

The Hamm-Saan remember every slight. We will return when this world offers something worthy of our song.

Farseer Calrad Faentar

Hamm-Saan Fleet
