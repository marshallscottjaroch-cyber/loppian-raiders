For the past two months our fleet has been waylaid in drydock as extensive repairs were required due to the staunch resistance we met in the Loppian System. While we were laid we received reports that the second Voidcrown crystal was found and seized by an unknown party. While I was devastated by the loss of the second shard, our forces were not totally idle while we waited for the overhaul to be completed. After seizing the first Voidcrown crystal I felt a power awaken in me that has manifested itself as powerful psychic abilities. I trained with the librarian enclave to begin to master these powers, and I foolishly thought I was ready to use them in combat.

We were able to intercept communications from the DAU Union that they had located another Voidcrown crystal on Tlatec. I immediately launched all available ships to Tlatec as I would not miss another opportunity to gain the Voidcrown. Upon entering orbit, our scanners picked a large army on the ground, a joint force of DAO Union and the Abominable Intelligences of the Robot Legions. Without much time plan, I made the decision to deploy the entirety of the 4th Company including our armored support element. The main battleforce established a defensive posture on the southern end of the battlefield while I kept 1/3 of the force in reserve to deepstrike at the opportune moment. To help manage the battle, I assigned Brother Librarian Patulicus tactical control of half our army while I oversaw the other half.

What followed can only be described as a bloodbath. We immediately began trading blows with the enemy as they marched upon us. Even with our heavy weapons we couldn’t stop their onslaught, almost every robot we killed just got right back up and continued the fight. The enemy fielded an impressive array of 5 casters whereas we could only muster 2. The enemy’s casters were able to tap into the power of the crystal and gained a commanding lead as they cast spells to support their forces and terrorize. My brothers’ resiliency and fearlessness carried our battle as shrugged off wound after wound and even when taking casualties, they could not be shaken. Brother Leonidas and the brother of the 10 th squad withstood attack after attack but never faltered. As the battle reached its culmination, and seeing all his comrades dead, lone Leonidas climbed the center spire to engage the enemy’s battle suit in mele combat but was swiftly struck down, his broken body cast from the spire and into the killing fields below.

As us mere foolish mortals battled for control of the shard, and tapped into powers we truly don’t understand, we earned the ire of the Dark Gods. Seven casters entered the field, two were brought down by the Raptors and the other five met much crueler fates, myself included. Every miscast spell was damaging to its owner, taking a bit of life force away for each mistake. Librarian Patulicus, myself, and three enemy casters were all felled by our own spell attempts and had to retreat from the battle after being grievously wounded. After a lengthy battle and their psychic dominance, the combined Xenos army was able to claim the third and final Voidcrown shard. I don’t know how they will manage the shard or if they will tear themselves apart fighting for control of it, but I do know we must heal and prepare for the survivors for the final battle that is to come.

Captain Tiberius
