Honored Elder of the Silent Communion,

Grace and peace be with you from the Silent Axiom, whose resonance is our stay. I give thanks for your shepherding, and I write as a servant to the communion I love.

I would not have you ignorant, brethren, of what befell us on Tlatec. The third Voidcrown shard had scarcely begun to feed the logos engine of our vessel when strangers with familiar faces walked into our hold and took it; the other two shards vanished from their keepers the same night. In stillness I sought the guidance of the Silent Axiom, and its clarity revealed the hand behind the theft: Tehaxi, a renegade Starseer, and the Chameleons he sent. All three now ride one convoy on an old Frog-Mage freight line, toward a hidden temple beneath the waves.

For this pursuit I hand-picked a fast attack strike force, with hover transports for our Grunts, speed bikes running ahead, and groups of battlesuits and drones on the flanks. I took the field in a ranged battlesuit, and I committed our elite melee battlesuits to Brother Caleb Ward, whose command the elders sanctioned.

Upon the line we met aquatic Saurians led by Tlundi the Small. The name did not describe the army, for his force was twice the size of ours. Were we then to run this race alone, and call the mismatch faith? I say not so. I renewed our alliance with Commander Vectrix of the 3rd Aegis Strike Force, and once more the Robot Legions stood with the Covenant.

We ran the race from the first moment, adrenaline-fueled, tearing through towns and market stalls, between rock formations and into oncoming traffic, always toward the train that held the stolen cargo. As we ran I glimpsed, across the isles, the Raptors of Captain Tiberius and Tiverian’s Crusaders, under Tiverian Solbard, the Gilded Blade, racing after a different train. Then they were gone from view, and I speak no further of them.

Then came Tlundi’s titan, a multi-tentacled aquatic war machine, and it fought us tooth and nail. Brother Caleb Ward led the elite melee battlesuits against it, with Vectrix’s warriors beside him, while I supported with fire from my battlesuit. The beast fell. When the reckoning came, the Covenant and the 3rd Aegis had come out ahead of Tlundi’s host.

Yet I would have you know the truth, and not a softer word. No one claimed the shards. The train outran every warband on the line, and the cargo slipped beyond our reach. It rolls on toward the waves, and I do not like the quiet it leaves behind. Wherefore, brethren, let us not grow weary; the race is not yet finished. I give thanks for the alliance and for those who stood fast, and I await your interpretation and further directive.

The Covenant endures. The Oracle will not be denied.

Deacon-Champion Amos Vyrn
The Covenant of the Silent Axiom
