“Our precious sacred relics were on board that train,” Tlundi muttered to himself, feeling smaller than usual. For Tlundi had shame far surmounting the startling defeat his forces suffered on that barren world. The night beforehand, the stars were aligned in the Saurians’ favor. Chutapec the Brave and Izeac the Wise, two star formations that rarely present themselves on the same night, shone brightly the night before, and Tlundi, the fool that he was, forgot to give prayer.

“Fool!” he yelled at himself. “No wonder none of our shots or blows landed. Cursed! We are cursed! And it is all my doing!” Tlundi cried. “No one must know my shame.”

Tears streaking down the tiny gecko’s cheeks, Tlundi curled himself into a ball. “Even with the help of our not-so-hive-minded friends, we still failed. I must redeem myself, and offer an abundance of sacrifices before our next battle.”
